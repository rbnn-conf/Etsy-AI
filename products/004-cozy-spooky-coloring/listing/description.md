# Cozy Spooky Halloween Coloring Book — 20 Connected Adventures

Follow a friendly ghost, a young witch, a black cat and their Halloween friends
through 20 cozy storytelling colouring pages. Bake treats, explore an autumn
forest and Halloween village, visit cafés and shops, join the celebrations,
then settle in for bedtime stories.

The collection shares a recurring world of friendly skeletons, bats, pumpkins,
haunted houses and seasonal activities. Each page is a new scene to colour,
arranged as a journey from Halloween preparations to the end of the night.

Your digital download includes:

- 20 unique colouring pages in an A4 PDF
- The same 20 pages in a US Letter PDF
- Individual printable PNG pages in both paper sizes
- A simple printing guide

Digital PDF and PNG files, designed for home printing. Download and extract
all supplied ZIP bundles to access the complete collection. Choose the paper
size that matches your printer and print one test page first.

This is a digital product. No physical item is shipped.
