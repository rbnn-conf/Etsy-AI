🖨️ HOW TO PRINT
1. Download and unzip all five folders after purchase.
2. Open the PDF that matches your paper, A4 or US Letter.
3. Print at 100% / Actual Size on standard white paper.
4. Print one test page first; if your printer needs larger margins, choose Fit to printable area.
