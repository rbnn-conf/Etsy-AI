# Midnight Séance — Prompt 2 design system

This is independent Product 3 design, based on the owner-supplied concept. No legacy visual module is imported or adapted. The approved 48-master scope is unchanged; only S01, S03, G03 and its G04 answer master are implemented for the three proof types.

Palette: near-black #171315, antique ivory #F4EBDD, oxblood #701F2A, plum noir #342434 and antique gold #A98A5B. Near-black is information ink; oxblood is title/focal colour; plum is a restrained secondary voice; gold is ornament only. Gold is ordinary printed colour, not foil. All information colours exceed 4.5:1 against their assigned ground; ornaments need not carry information.

Signature Ivory uses a clean flat ivory panel, oxblood headings and a 0.6 pt oxblood/gold double frame. No simulated ageing, grain or gradients obscure text. Economy White removes the ivory fill and inner frame, uses near-black headings and restrained brown-grey ornament, and retains the same physical layout, type hierarchy, text and puzzle.

Typography: Bodoni Moda display/italic + Source Sans 3 utility. See the local font record and licence files. Physical spacing scale is 1/2/3/5/8/13/21 mm. Home carrier content uses 10 mm margins. Invitation uses 14 mm text inset; native stationery frame stays 6 mm inside trim. Small utility text remains 8.5 pt or above. Thin activity cell rules are secondary guides; the outer grid and decoration use 0.6 pt rules.

Components: product-owned page shell, title/kicker, editable field, divider, decorative corner frame, crescent, moon phases, activity grid and word/check list. These are deterministic HTML/CSS/SVG. Customer-facing wording and puzzle letters remain real selectable text in the PDFs.

Invitation is 127 × 177.8 mm (5 × 7 in) and stays that size when centered on A4/Letter carriers. Welcome master is 203.2 × 254 mm (8 × 10 in); A4/Letter editions are reflowed into 190 × 277 mm / 195.9 × 259.4 mm panels with 10 mm carrier margins, not distorted or enlarged invitation copies. Word search uses a 16 × 16 grid, 8.2 mm cells and 18 terms.

Editing is constrained text only. Character limits are explicit, not unlimited promises. Longer titles switch to documented display sizes; text wraps with no ellipsis/truncation. Unsupported/over-limit/missing required fields and geometry failures disable printing. An invalid edit invalidates earlier pending renders to avoid stale ready states. Optional lines can be absent. UK/US and accented/currency fixtures are supplied and tested.

Final illustration slots remain replaceable and empty until Prompt 3. No proof is called final artwork. On approval, Prompt 3 adds original botanical/lace/apothecary assets to this system and reruns the three proof types; no further pages or marketing are authorized by this milestone.
