The reference positions ivory stationery against black lace and oxblood velvet. Its identity comes from high-contrast editorial capitals, symmetrical title hierarchy, engraved botanical corners, sparse gold celestial marks and generous separation between ornament and information. Activities and tables have much lighter ornament than the invitation. The dominant light paper is essential to home-print usability; the central dark hero is marketing context, not a requirement for black printable pages.

Translate: ivory grounds, oxblood focal titles, near-black utility text, understated plum italic voice and antique-gold linework; coordinated but differentiated page hierarchy. Invitation uses compact summons/event/details ordering. Welcome uses a generous crescent, large event axis and separate host/motto zones. Activity uses a small running collection name, editorial mixed-case title, plain instructions and a clear grid.

Do not translate: paper staining, generated spelling, malformed hands/illustrations, the LumiumX wordmark, skull/crystal-ball scene, photographed props or literal botanical tracing.
