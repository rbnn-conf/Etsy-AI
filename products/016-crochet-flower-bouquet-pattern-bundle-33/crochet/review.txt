01  Garden Rose  (garden-rose)
Flower · intermediate · About 8 cm across
Yarn: Smooth cotton or cotton-blend yarn for the rose bloom, Dusty blush pink, About 15 g (2-fine)
Hook: 2.5 mm (US B/1)
Also needed: Tapestry needle, Scissors
Gauge: Gauge is not critical because this is a decorative flower. Keep your tension even so the petal layers sit neatly together.
Abbreviations: ch = chain; sl st = slip stitch; sc = single crochet; hdc = half double crochet; dc = double crochet; tr = treble crochet; MR = magic ring; Rnd = round; rep = repeat; sk = skip

Centre
  Row 1: Ch 20. Starting in 2nd ch from hook, sc in next ch, hdc in next ch, 2 dc in each of next 15 ch, hdc in next ch, sc in last ch. (34)

Inner Petal Ring
  Rnd 1: Make an MR, work 6 sc in MR, and join with a sl st in first sc. (6)
  Rnd 2: *(Ch 3, sk next sc, sl st in next sc) rep 2 times. Three chain loops are made.
  Rnd 3: Sl st into first chain loop; ch 1, then work (sc, hdc, 2 dc, hdc, sc) in the same loop. *Sl st into next chain loop, then work (sc, hdc, 2 dc, hdc, sc) in that loop; rep 2 times. Join with a sl st in first sc of Rnd 3. (18)

Medium Petal Ring
  Rnd 1: Make an MR, work 8 sc in MR, and join with a sl st in first sc. (8)
  Rnd 2: *(Ch 4, sk next sc, sl st in next sc) rep 3 times. Four chain loops are made.
  Rnd 3: Sl st into first chain loop; ch 1, then work (sc, hdc, 3 dc, hdc, sc) in the same loop. *Sl st into next chain loop, then work (sc, hdc, 3 dc, hdc, sc) in that loop; rep 3 times. Join with a sl st in first sc of Rnd 3. (28)

Outer Petal Ring
  Rnd 1: Make an MR, work 10 sc in MR, and join with a sl st in first sc. (10)
  Rnd 2: *(Ch 5, sk next sc, sl st in next sc) rep 4 times. Five chain loops are made.
  Rnd 3: Sl st into first chain loop; ch 1, then work (sc, hdc, 2 dc, tr, 2 dc, hdc, sc) in the same loop. *Sl st into next chain loop, then work (sc, hdc, 2 dc, tr, 2 dc, hdc, sc) in that loop; rep 4 times. Join with a sl st in first sc of Rnd 3. (40)

Assembly
  1. Roll the Centre strip from its first sc end into a tight spiral, keeping the fuller dc section along the upper edge. Use its reserved yarn tail to make four small stitches through the base of the roll and secure the spiral.
  2. Lay the Outer Petal Ring flat. Centre the Medium Petal Ring over it, offsetting the petals so each medium petal sits between two outer petals.
  3. Place the Inner Petal Ring over the Medium Petal Ring, again offsetting its petals. Align all three MR openings and use reserved yarn tails to sew the layers together through the openings.
  4. Position the rolled Centre over the Inner Petal Ring, covering the MR opening. Sew securely through the base of the roll and the inner ring without stitching through visible petal edges.

Finishing
  1. After crocheting each piece, fasten off, leaving a 20 cm yarn tail on the Centre and each petal ring for sewing.
  2. After assembly, weave in all remaining yarn tails on the reverse side without pulling the centre or petals out of shape.
  3. Gently spread and curve the petals with your fingers, arranging the inner petals upright and the outer petals slightly outward.

Notes
  - For a fuller bloom, keep the petal rings loosely sewn so the petals can overlap naturally.
  - Use the same rose colour throughout, or make the Centre one shade deeper for a softly shaded flower.

02  Moon Peony  (moon-peony)
flower · experienced · About 11 cm across; finished wired stem is about 28 cm long.
Yarn: Mercerised cotton yarn for layered petals, Moonlit ivory, About 20 g | Mercerised cotton yarn for padded centre, Pale butter yellow, About 3 g | Mercerised cotton yarn for calyx and stem wrap, Soft sage green, About 4 g (2-fine)
Hook: 2.5 mm (US B-1/C-2)
Also needed: Polyester fibrefill, small amount, Tapestry needle, Scissors, One 35 cm length of 24-gauge floral wire, Green floral tape
Gauge: Gauge is not critical. Use a firm tension so the petal edges hold their cupped shape and the padded centre remains neat.
Abbreviations: ch = chain; sl st = slip stitch; sc = single crochet; hdc = half double crochet; dc = double crochet; tr = treble crochet; dtr = double treble crochet; st = stitch; sts = stitches; rnd = round; rnds = rounds; rep = repeat; MR = magic ring; inc = work 2 sc in the same stitch

Padded Centre Discs (make 2)
  Rnd 1: Make an MR, ch 1, work 6 sc in the MR; join with sl st to the first sc. (6)
  Rnd 2: Ch 1, inc in each st around; join with sl st to the first sc. (12)
  Finish: Fasten off each disc, leaving a 30 cm yarn tail for seaming.

Small Petal Ring (make 1)
  Rnd 1: Make an MR, ch 1, work 6 sc in the MR; join with sl st to the first sc. (6)
  Rnd 2: Ch 1, inc in each st around; join with sl st to the first sc. (12)
  Rnd 3: Work (sc, hdc, 3 dc, hdc, sc) in the next st, sl st in the next st; rep from the beginning of this instruction 5 more times to make 6 petals. (48)
  Finish: Fasten off, leaving a 15 cm yarn tail for assembly.

Medium Petal Ring (make 1)
  Rnd 1: Make an MR, ch 1, work 6 sc in the MR; join with sl st to the first sc. (6)
  Rnd 2: Ch 1, inc in each st around; join with sl st to the first sc. (12)
  Rnd 3: Ch 1, work (sc in the next st, inc in the next st) 6 times; join with sl st to the first sc. (18)
  Rnd 4: Work (sc, hdc, dc, 3 tr, dc, hdc, sc) in the next st, sl st in the next st; rep from the beginning of this instruction 8 more times to make 9 petals. (90)
  Finish: Fasten off, leaving a 15 cm yarn tail for assembly.

Large Petal Ring (make 1)
  Rnd 1: Make an MR, ch 1, work 6 sc in the MR; join with sl st to the first sc. (6)
  Rnd 2: Ch 1, inc in each st around; join with sl st to the first sc. (12)
  Rnd 3: Ch 1, sc in the next 2 sts, then work (sc in the next st, inc in the next st) 6 times; join with sl st to the first sc. (20)
  Rnd 4: Work (sc, hdc, dc, 2 tr, dtr, 2 tr, dc, hdc, sc) in the next st, sl st in the next st; rep from the beginning of this instruction 9 more times to make 10 petals. (120)
  Finish: Fasten off, leaving a 15 cm yarn tail for assembly.

Outer Petal Ring (make 1)
  Rnd 1: Make an MR, ch 1, work 6 sc in the MR; join with sl st to the first sc. (6)
  Rnd 2: Ch 1, inc in each st around; join with sl st to the first sc. (12)
  Rnd 3: Ch 1, work (sc in the next st, inc in the next st) 6 times; join with sl st to the first sc. (18)
  Rnd 4: Ch 1, work (sc in the next st, inc in the next st) 6 times; join with sl st to the first sc. (24)
  Rnd 5: Work (sc, hdc, dc, 2 tr, 2 dtr, 2 tr, dc, hdc, sc) in the next st, sl st in the next st; rep from the beginning of this instruction 11 more times to make 12 petals. (156)
  Finish: Fasten off, leaving a 20 cm yarn tail for assembly.

Calyx (make 1)
  Rnd 1: With sage green yarn, make an MR, ch 1, work 5 sc in the MR; join with sl st to the first sc. (5)
  Rnd 2: Ch 1, inc in each st around; join with sl st to the first sc. (10)
  Rnd 3: Work (sc, hdc, dc, tr, ch 3, sl st in the third ch from hook, tr, dc, hdc, sc) in the next st, sl st in the next st; rep from the beginning of this instruction 4 more times to make 5 pointed sepals. (50)
  Finish: Fasten off, leaving a 20 cm yarn tail for assembly.

Assembly
  1. Place the two centre discs with their outside faces facing outward. Whip stitch through corresponding outer stitches, using one centre tail; add small amounts of fibrefill before closing the final 3 stitches.
  2. Place the Outer Petal Ring beneath the padded centre with its petal side facing upward. Use its tail to sew every second base stitch to the underside of the centre, making 12 evenly spaced attachment points.
  3. Place the Large Petal Ring above the outer ring and beneath the centre. Sew every second base stitch to the underside of the centre with its tail, making 10 evenly spaced attachment points.
  4. Place the Medium Petal Ring above the large ring and beneath the centre. Sew every second base stitch to the underside of the centre with its tail, making 9 evenly spaced attachment points.
  5. Place the Small Petal Ring nearest the centre. Sew every second base stitch to the underside of the centre with its tail, making 6 evenly spaced attachment points.
  6. Centre the calyx beneath all petal-ring bases, with the sepals pointing outward. Sew its centre securely to the underside of the flower using its yarn tail.
  7. Bend one end of the floral wire into a 2 cm hook. Lay the hook flat against the calyx centre and secure it with several firm stitches of sage yarn through the calyx and outer petal-ring base.
  8. Wrap the full wire length with green floral tape, overlapping the tape slightly. Begin at the calyx and stretch the tape gently as it is wrapped down the stem.

Finishing
  1. Fasten off any remaining sewing yarn by passing it through several underside stitches and pulling it snugly closed.
  2. Weave all yarn tails into the underside of the flower or through the wrapped stem, keeping them away from the visible petal faces.
  3. Gently curve the petals upward from their bases and soften the outer petal edges with your fingers to create a full, ruffled bloom.
  4. Bend the wired stem gradually into the desired bouquet position. Avoid sharp bends that could crease the floral tape.

Notes
  - The slip stitches between petals create separations and encourage the petals to cup upward.
  - For a fuller bloom, stagger each petal ring so a petal sits in front of the gap between two petals on the layer beneath.
  - Keep the centre lightly stuffed; overfilling can make the petal layers stand too far away from the bloom.

03  Heirloom Dahlia  (heirloom-dahlia)
flower · experienced · About 10 cm across with a stem about 20 cm long.
Yarn: Mercerised cotton yarn for the layered dahlia petals, Deep plum, mauve, or moonlit pink, About 25 g | Mercerised cotton yarn for calyx and stem, Leaf green, About 10 g (2-fine)
Hook: 2.5 mm (US B-1/C-2)
Also needed: 24-gauge floral wire, 24 cm long, Floral tape, Tapestry needle, Stitch marker, Wire cutters or strong pliers, Scissors
Gauge: Gauge is not critical. Using the stated yarn and hook gives a bloom about 10 cm across.
Abbreviations: ch = chain; sl st = slip stitch; sc = single crochet; hdc = half double crochet; dc = double crochet; tr = treble crochet; st = stitch; sts = stitches; rnd = round; rnds = rounds; MR = magic ring; FLO = front loop only; BLO = back loop only; inc = work 2 sc in the same stitch

Dahlia Bloom
  Rnd 1: Ch 1, work 8 sc in an MR; join with sl st in first sc. The ch 1 does not count as a st. (8)
  Rnd 2: Working in the FLO of each Rnd 1 st, make 8 petals: sl st in FLO, ch 4, sl st in second ch from hook, sc in next ch, hdc in next ch, then sl st in the same FLO.
  Rnd 3: Ch 1, working in the BLO of Rnd 1, inc in each st around; join with sl st in first sc. The ch 1 does not count as a st. (16)
  Rnd 4: Working in the FLO of each Rnd 3 st, make 16 petals: sl st in FLO, ch 5, sl st in second ch from hook, sc in next ch, hdc in next ch, dc in next ch, then sl st in the same FLO.
  Rnd 5: Ch 1, working in the BLO of Rnd 3, work sc in next st, inc in next st 8 times; join with sl st in first sc. The ch 1 does not count as a st. (24)
  Rnd 6: Working in the FLO of each Rnd 5 st, make 24 petals: sl st in FLO, ch 7, sl st in second ch from hook, sc in next 2 ch, hdc in next 2 ch, dc in next ch, then sl st in the same FLO.
  Rnd 7: Ch 1, working in the BLO of Rnd 5, work sc in each of next 2 sts, inc in next st 8 times; join with sl st in first sc. The ch 1 does not count as a st. (32)
  Rnd 8: Working in the FLO of each Rnd 7 st, make 32 petals: sl st in FLO, ch 9, sl st in second ch from hook, sc in next 2 ch, hdc in next 2 ch, dc in next 3 ch, then sl st in the same FLO.
  Rnd 9: Ch 1, working in the BLO of Rnd 7, work sc in each of next 3 sts, inc in next st 8 times; join with sl st in first sc. The ch 1 does not count as a st. (40)
  Rnd 10: Working in the FLO of each Rnd 9 st, make 40 petals: sl st in FLO, ch 12, sl st in second ch from hook, sc in next 2 ch, hdc in next 3 ch, dc in next 3 ch, tr in next 2 ch, then sl st in the same FLO.

Calyx
  Rnd 1: With green yarn, ch 1, work 6 sc in an MR; join with sl st in first sc. The ch 1 does not count as a st. (6)
  Rnd 2: Ch 1, inc in each st around; join with sl st in first sc. The ch 1 does not count as a st. (12)
  Rnd 3: Ch 1, work sc in next st, inc in next st 6 times; join with sl st in first sc. The ch 1 does not count as a st. (18)
  Rnd 4: Make 6 sepals: sl st in next st, ch 7, sl st in second ch from hook, sc in next 2 ch, hdc in next 2 ch, dc in next ch, sl st in the same st, then sl st in each of next 2 sts.

Stem
  Rnd 1: With green yarn, ch 1, work 5 sc in an MR; join with sl st in first sc. The ch 1 does not count as a st. (5)
  Rnds 2-100: Ch 1, sc in each st around; join with sl st in first sc. The ch 1 does not count as a st. (5)

Assembly
  1. Cut a 24 cm length of floral wire. Insert it through the stem tube, leaving 3 cm extending from the top. Trim any wire extending from the lower end flush with the tube, then bend the trimmed tip inward with pliers.
  2. Bend the upper 3 cm of wire into a flat loop about 1 cm across. Use the stem fastening-off tail to lash the loop securely to the centre of the calyx with six passes through the calyx MR.
  3. Place the wrong side of the bloom against the calyx, with the calyx centred beneath the bloom. Use the bloom tail to sew the calyx edge to the underside at the bases of the outer petals with 12 evenly spaced whipstitches.
  4. Wrap floral tape from the calyx down the upper 3 cm of stem, covering the wire loop and securing the calyx neatly against the stem.

Finishing
  1. Fasten off the bloom after Rnd 10, leaving a 35 cm yarn tail for attaching it to the calyx.
  2. Fasten off the calyx after Rnd 4, leaving a 20 cm yarn tail for securing it to the wire loop.
  3. Fasten off the stem after Rnd 100, leaving a 20 cm yarn tail for securing the wire loop.
  4. Weave in the magic-ring tails and all remaining yarn tails on the reverse side after assembly, taking care not to distort the petals.
  5. Gently spread the petal layers from the centre outward. Curve the outer petals slightly forward to create a full, rounded dahlia shape.
  6. Check that all wire ends are enclosed or bent inward before displaying or gifting the flower.

Notes
  - The petal rounds are worked in the front loops of the preceding structural round. Each following structural round is worked in the unused back loops of the stated earlier round.
  - For a darker moonlit centre, work Rnds 1-4 in a deeper shade of the petal yarn, then change to the main petal shade at Rnd 5.

04  Meadow Ranunculus  (meadow-ranunculus)
Flower · intermediate · About 6 centimetres across, with an optional 20-centimetre wired stem.
Yarn: Fine cotton yarn for layered petals, Pale blush pink, About 8 grams | Fine cotton yarn for rounded centre, Warm golden yellow, About 2 grams (2-fine)
Hook: 2.25 mm (US B-1)
Also needed: Polyester fibre fill, Tapestry needle, Stitch marker, Scissors, One 20-centimetre length of 22-gauge floral wire, Floral tape
Gauge: Gauge is not critical. Fine yarn and the stated hook create delicate petals; finished size varies with wrapping tension.
Abbreviations: ch = chain; sl st = slip stitch; sc = single crochet; hdc = half double crochet; dc = double crochet; st = stitch; sts = stitches; Rnd = round; MR = magic ring; inc = work 2 sc in the same st; dec = single crochet decrease over 2 sts; FO = fasten off

Rounded Centre
  Rnd 1: With golden yellow yarn, make an MR. Ch 1, work 6 sc in the MR, then sl st to the first sc to join. Pull the MR tight. The ch 1 does not count as a st. (6)
  Rnd 2: Ch 1, work an inc in every st around, then sl st to the first sc to join. The ch 1 does not count as a st. (12)
  Rnd 3: Ch 1; six times, work sc in the next st and an inc in the next st. Sl st to the first sc to join. The ch 1 does not count as a st. (18)
  Rnd 4: Ch 1, sc in every st around, then sl st to the first sc to join. The ch 1 does not count as a st. (18)
  Rnd 5: Ch 1; six times, work sc in the next st and a dec over the following 2 sts. Sl st to the first sc to join. The ch 1 does not count as a st. (12)
  After Rnd 5: Stuff the centre firmly with small pieces of polyester fibre fill, keeping the shape rounded.
  Rnd 6: Ch 1; six times, work a dec over the next 2 sts. Sl st to the first sc to join. The ch 1 does not count as a st. (6)
  Close: FO, leaving a 25-centimetre tail. Thread the tail through the front loops of the 6 remaining sts, pull closed, and secure with one small stitch.

Inner Petal Strip
  Preparation: With pale blush pink yarn, leave a 25-centimetre starting tail before making the slip knot.
  Row 1: Ch 21. Starting in the second ch from the hook, work this sequence four times: sc in the next ch, hdc in the next ch, dc in the next ch, hdc in the next ch, sc in the next ch. FO, leaving a 25-centimetre tail. (20)

Middle Petal Strip
  Preparation: With pale blush pink yarn, leave a 25-centimetre starting tail before making the slip knot.
  Row 1: Ch 31. Starting in the second ch from the hook, work this sequence six times: sc in the next ch, hdc in the next ch, 2 dc in the next ch, hdc in the next ch, sc in the next ch. FO, leaving a 25-centimetre tail. (36)

Outer Petal Strip
  Preparation: With pale blush pink yarn, leave a 25-centimetre starting tail before making the slip knot.
  Row 1: Ch 41. Starting in the second ch from the hook, work this sequence eight times: sc in the next ch, hdc in the next ch, 3 dc in the next ch, hdc in the next ch, sc in the next ch. FO, leaving a 25-centimetre tail. (56)

Assembly
  1. Bend the upper 1 centimetre of the floral wire into a narrow U-shaped hook. Centre this hook over the closed end of the rounded centre. With the centre tail, sew through the 6 sts of Rnd 6 and around the wire hook six times; pull firmly and knot securely.
  2. Place the Inner Petal Strip with its scalloped edge facing upwards and its chain edge against Rnd 5 of the centre. Sew the starting edge to Rnd 5, then wrap the strip once around the centre. Tack the chain edge to Rnd 5 after petal 2 and petal 4, then sew the final edge to the starting edge.
  3. Position the Middle Petal Strip around the inner layer, beginning one petal to the left of the inner-strip seam. Keep its chain edge at the base. Wrap it once, then tack through its chain edge, Rnd 5, and the inner strip after petals 2, 4, and 6. Sew its final edge to its starting edge.
  4. Position the Outer Petal Strip around the previous layers, with its starting edge one petal to the left of the middle-strip seam. Wrap it once with the chain edge at the base. Tack through all layers after petals 2, 4, 6, and 8, then sew the final edge to its starting edge.
  5. Stretch floral tape slightly and wrap it from the flower base down the wire in overlapping turns. Cover the wire completely and press the tape end firmly in place.

Finishing
  1. Fasten off any yarn still attached by cutting the yarn, pulling the tail through the final loop, and tightening it securely.
  2. Weave in all remaining yarn tails on the underside of the flower, taking care not to pull the petal layers out of position.
  3. Cup the petals upwards around the centre with your fingers, arranging the outer petals slightly more open than the inner petals.
  4. Bend the lower wire tip back on itself and cover it with floral tape so that no sharp end is exposed.

Notes
  - The five- or six-chain foundation section forming each repeated sequence is one petal. The repeated dc stitches make the petal edges naturally ruffled.
  - For a fuller bloom, overlap neighbouring petals slightly while making the tacking stitches; for a more open flower, space the tacking stitches around the base more evenly.

05  Starlit Anemone  (starlit-anemone)
flower · easy · About 7 cm across
Yarn: Fine cotton or cotton-blend yarn for petals, Dusky periwinkle, Approx. 4 g | Fine cotton or cotton-blend yarn for centre, Midnight charcoal, Approx. 1 g (2-fine)
Hook: 2.5 mm (US B-1)
Also needed: Tapestry needle, Sharp scissors, Small pins and blocking mat, optional
Gauge: Gauge is not critical because this is a decorative flower. Use a firm tension so the centre holds its shape.
Abbreviations: ch = chain; sl st = slip stitch; sc = single crochet; hdc = half double crochet; dc = double crochet; hdc dec = half double crochet decrease: work 2 hdc together; bobble = 4-dc bobble: work 4 partial dc in one stitch, then close all loops; MR = magic ring; rnd = round; rep = repeat; inc = increase: work 2 sc in the same stitch; yo = yarn over

Textured Centre
  Rnd 1: With centre yarn, make an MR. Ch 1, work 6 sc in the MR, then join with sl st to the first sc. (6)
  Rnd 2: Ch 1, inc in each sc around. Join with sl st to the first sc. (12)
  Rnd 3: Ch 1, *sc in next stitch, bobble in next stitch; rep from * 5 more times. Push each bobble to the right side. Join with sl st to the first sc. (12)

Petals (make 6)
  Foundation: With petal yarn, ch 4.
  Row 1: Sc in second ch from hook, hdc in next ch, dc in last ch. Turn. (3)
  Row 2: Ch 2, work 2 dc in first stitch, dc in next stitch, 2 dc in last stitch. Turn. (5)
  Row 3: Ch 2, work 2 dc in first stitch, dc in each of the next 3 stitches, 2 dc in last stitch. Turn. (7)
  Row 4: Ch 2, work 2 dc in first stitch, dc in each of the next 5 stitches, 2 dc in last stitch. Turn. (9)
  Row 5: Ch 1, sc in each stitch across. Turn. (9)
  Row 6: Ch 2, hdc dec over first 2 stitches, hdc in each of the next 5 stitches, hdc dec over last 2 stitches. Turn. (7)
  Row 7: Ch 2, hdc dec over first 2 stitches, hdc in each of the next 3 stitches, hdc dec over last 2 stitches. Turn. (5)
  Row 8: Ch 2, hdc dec over first 2 stitches, hdc in next stitch, hdc dec over last 2 stitches. Turn. (3)
  Row 9: Ch 1, sc in each stitch across. Leave the working loop in place for fastening off. (3)

Assembly
  1. Fasten off each petal as described in Finishing, retaining its 20 cm yarn tail. Place the centre right side up, with the bobbles facing front.
  2. Position one petal behind each sc from Rnd 3 of the centre. Arrange the 3-stitch Row 1 edge of every petal against the centre so the petals radiate evenly.
  3. Using each petal's retained tail, sew through all 3 stitches of its Row 1 edge and through the corresponding sc of Rnd 3. Secure each petal with two small stitches on the reverse side.

Finishing
  1. Fasten off the centre after Rnd 3, leaving a 10 cm tail. Fasten off each petal after Row 9, leaving a 20 cm tail: cut yarn, pull the tail through the final loop, and tighten.
  2. After assembly, weave the centre tail and all petal sewing tails through nearby reverse-side stitches. Trim the tails close without cutting the fabric.
  3. Lightly block the petals into a gentle open cup shape. Pin the petals evenly, mist lightly if suitable for the yarn, and allow them to dry completely.

Notes
  - Turning chains do not count as stitches throughout.
  - To make a bobble, yo, insert hook in the indicated stitch, yo and pull up a loop, yo and pull through 2 loops. Rep this partial dc 3 more times in the same stitch, then yo and pull through all 5 loops on the hook.

06  Cottage Poppy  (cottage-poppy)
flower · intermediate · About 9 cm across, with an about 18 cm wired stem.
Yarn: Mercerised cotton yarn, softly ruffled petal colour, Dusky coral red, About 12 g | Mercerised cotton yarn, centre, calyx and stem colour, Sage green, About 8 g | Cotton embroidery floss for seed-head spokes, Charcoal, About 1 m (2-fine)
Hook: 2.5 mm (US B/1)
Also needed: 20 cm length of 22-gauge floral wire, Small amount of polyester fibrefill, Tapestry needle, Scissors, Small pliers for bending wire
Gauge: Gauge is not critical because this is a decorative wired flower. Keep your tension even so the petals retain their light, open shape.
Abbreviations: BLO = back loop only; ch = chain; dc = double crochet; hdc = half double crochet; MR = magic ring; rep = repeat; rnd = round; sc = single crochet; sc2tog = single crochet two stitches together; sk = skip; sl st = slip stitch; st = stitch; sts = stitches; tr = treble crochet

Petal (make 4)
  Row 1: With petal yarn, ch 2. Work 6 sc in the second ch from hook; turn. (6)
  Row 2: Ch 1, which does not count as a st. Work 2 sc in first st, sc in each of the next 4 sts, and 2 sc in last st; turn. (8)
  Row 3: Ch 1, which does not count as a st. Work 2 hdc in first st, hdc in each of the next 6 sts, and 2 hdc in last st; turn. (10)
  Row 4: Ch 2, which does not count as a st. Work 2 dc in first st, dc in each of the next 8 sts, and 2 dc in last st; turn. (12)
  Row 5: Ch 3, which does not count as a st. Work 2 tr in first st, tr in each of the next 10 sts, and 2 tr in last st; turn. (14)
  Row 6: Ch 1, which does not count as a st. Sc in first st, then rep the following 6 times: ch 3, sk next st, sc in next st. Ch 3, sk last st, and sl st in first sc to complete the final loop. Cut yarn, leaving a 15 cm sewing tail, and fasten off. (7)

Seed-Head Centre (make 1)
  Rnd 1: With sage yarn, make an MR, leaving a 25 cm starting tail for sewing. Work 6 sc in MR; pull closed. (6)
  Rnd 2: Work 2 sc in each st around. (12)
  Rnd 3: Rep the following 6 times: sc in next st, 2 sc in next st. (18)
  Rnd 4: Working in BLO, sc in each st around. (18)
  Rnd 5: Sc in each st around. (18)
  Rnd 6: Rep the following 6 times: sc in next st, sc2tog. (12)
  Rnd 7: Work sc2tog 5 times. Stuff the centre firmly with fibrefill, then work sc2tog once more. Cut yarn and fasten off securely. (6)

Calyx (make 1)
  Rnd 1: With sage yarn, make an MR. Rep the following 5 times: sc in MR, ch 5, sl st in second ch from hook, sc in each of the next 3 ch. Sl st in first sc to join. Do not pull the MR fully closed; leave a small opening for the wire. Cut yarn, leaving a 20 cm sewing tail, and fasten off. (5)

Stem Cover (make 1)
  Row 1: With sage yarn, ch 35. Sc in second ch from hook and in each remaining ch; turn. (34)
  Row 2: Ch 1, which does not count as a st. Sc in each st across; turn. (34)
  Row 3: Ch 1, which does not count as a st. Sc in each st across; turn. (34)
  Row 4: Ch 1, which does not count as a st. Sc in each st across. Cut yarn, leaving a 15 cm sewing tail, and fasten off. (34)

Assembly
  1. Using charcoal embroidery floss, embroider 8 evenly spaced straight spokes on the closed Rnd 7 side of the seed-head centre. Bring each spoke from the centre of Rnd 7 out to the ridge made by Rnd 4.
